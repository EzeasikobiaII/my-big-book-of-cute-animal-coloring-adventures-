# Page 5: Happy Puppy at the Park

## Coloring Page Concept

A cute, happy puppy enjoying a fun day at the park.

The puppy should be the main focus of the illustration and should have a
friendly, playful expression.

## Main Character

A young, adorable puppy sitting on the grass.

The puppy should have:

- Large friendly eyes
- Floppy ears
- A small smiling mouth
- A cheerful expression
- A simple collar without any logo or brand

## Adventure Setting

Create a simple park scene around the puppy.

Include:

- A large ball beside the puppy
- A small tree
- A few large flowers
- Simple grass
- Two or three fluffy clouds
- A small butterfly

Keep the background simple so the puppy remains the main focus.

## Composition

Place the puppy prominently in the center of the page.

The ball should be close to the puppy.

The tree and flowers should provide a simple sense of location without
crowding the page.

Leave generous open areas for coloring.

## Illustration Style

- Cute children's coloring-book style
- Black-and-white line art
- Bold, smooth outlines
- Large coloring areas
- Simple shapes
- Friendly facial expression
- Minimal detail
- No gray shading
- No gradients
- White background

## Audience

Children ages 4–8.

## Print Specifications

- Trim size: 8.5 × 11 inches
- Portrait orientation
- No bleed
- Target resolution: 300 DPI
- Target artwork size: 2550 × 3300 pixels
- Keep all important artwork safely inside the page margins

## Restrictions

Do not include:

- Copyrighted characters
- Brand logos
- Trademarked designs
- Watermarks
- Photorealistic elements
- Scary imagery
- Extremely thin lines
- Tiny coloring areas
- Unnecessary text
