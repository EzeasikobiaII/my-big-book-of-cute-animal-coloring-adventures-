---

## 2. Book Concept

A fun and engaging coloring adventure featuring 50 cute animals in simple, imaginative settings, followed by creative activities that encourage children to draw, observe, match, and explore the animal world.

The illustrations must be friendly, recognizable, and easy for children ages 4–8 to color.

The finished book should provide an enjoyable coloring experience while helping children explore different animals, their distinctive features, and their natural environments.

---

## 3. Illustration Style

Every coloring illustration must use:

- Bold, clean black outlines.
- Large, open coloring areas.
- Simple, recognizable shapes.
- Cute and friendly animal expressions.
- Spacious, uncluttered backgrounds.
- Clear separation between objects.
- White backgrounds with black line art only.
- No gray shading or gradients.
- No unnecessary tiny details.
- No copyrighted characters.
- No brand logos or trademarks.
- No frightening or inappropriate imagery.
- No unnecessary solid-black areas that prevent children from coloring.

Artwork must be designed specifically for children ages 4–8.

### 3.1 Line-Art Requirements

1. Use clear black outlines.
2. Keep the background white.
3. Avoid grayscale fills, gradients, and heavy shading.
4. Avoid excessive hatching, stippling, and tiny decorative marks.
5. Maintain sufficient white space for crayons and colored pencils.
6. Ensure the artwork remains clear when printed.
7. Use consistent line weight throughout each page.
8. Keep all important artwork within the safe printing area.

### 3.2 Child-Friendly Composition

Each illustration should:

- Feature one primary animal.
- Make the animal large enough to color comfortably.
- Show a recognizable and appropriate animal pose.
- Use a friendly, approachable expression.
- Include only a reasonable number of background objects.
- Separate overlapping objects clearly.
- Avoid overly complicated scenes.

The animal must remain the main focus of the page.

---

## 4. Permanent Header and Lettering Rules

**These rules are mandatory for all 50 animal coloring pages.**

### 4.1 Standard Page Header

Every animal coloring page must use this exact heading:

**COLOR • IMAGINE • HAVE FUN!**

The header must follow these requirements:

- Every letter must have a bold black outline.
- Every letter must have a white, unshaded interior.
- Children must be able to color inside every letter.
- No header letter may be filled with solid black.
- No gray fills, gradients, or shading may appear inside the letters
