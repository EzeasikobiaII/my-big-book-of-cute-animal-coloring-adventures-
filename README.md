---

## Book Concept

A fun and engaging coloring adventure featuring 50 cute animals in simple, imaginative settings, followed by creative activities that encourage children to draw, observe, match, and explore the animal world.

The illustrations should be friendly, recognizable, and easy for children ages 4–8 to color.

---

## Illustration Style

Every coloring illustration should use:

- Bold, clean black outlines
- Large, open coloring areas
- Simple, recognizable shapes
- Cute and friendly animal expressions
- Spacious, uncluttered backgrounds
- Clear separation between objects
- White background with black line art only
- No gray shading or gradients
- No unnecessary tiny details
- No copyrighted characters
- No brand logos or trademarks
- No frightening or inappropriate imagery

Animal names on the main coloring pages should appear below the animal in large, bold outlined letters with white interiors so children can color them. Do not use solid-black animal-name lettering.

Artwork should be designed specifically for children ages 4–8.
