# Page 59: Animal Fun Facts

## Activity
Create a fun educational activity page where children can learn simple and interesting facts about animals.

## Page Layout
Include:

**ANIMAL FUN FACTS**

Place the heading near the top of the page using large, bold, outlined letters with white/open interiors so children can color the letters.

Below the heading, create five large, rounded fact boxes.

Each box should contain one simple child-friendly animal fact:

**1.**
Elephants use their trunks to pick up food and drink water.

**2.**
Penguins cannot fly, but they are excellent swimmers.

**3.**
Giraffes have very long necks that help them reach leaves high in trees.

**4.**
Otters can float on their backs while resting in the water.

**5.**
Kangaroos use their powerful back legs to hop across the ground.

Add small, simple decorative animal footprints and stars around the fact boxes.

Keep the text large, clear, and easy for children ages 4–8 to read with help from an adult.

## Illustration Style
- Black-and-white children's activity-book line art
- Bold, smooth outlines
- Large open coloring areas
- Simple decorative shapes
- Clear separation between fact boxes
- No gray
- No gradients
- No shading
- White background
- Plenty of open space
- Easy for children ages 4–8 to use

## Audience
Kids ages 4–8.

## Print Specifications
- Trim size: 8.5 × 11 inches
- Orientation: Portrait
- Interior: Black-and-white
- Bleed: None
- Target resolution: 300 DPI
- Target artwork dimensions: 2550 × 3300 pixels
- Keep all important artwork and text inside the safe area

## Content Restrictions
Do not include:
- Copyrighted characters
- Trademarked characters
- Logos
- Brand names
- Watermarks
- Photorealistic imagery
- Scary or frightening imagery
- Weapons
- Tiny text
- Crowded layouts
- Excessive decorative elements
- Solid black decorative lettering

## Quality Goal
The finished page should feel like a fun educational break from coloring, giving children a few simple animal facts while still providing decorative areas they can color.
