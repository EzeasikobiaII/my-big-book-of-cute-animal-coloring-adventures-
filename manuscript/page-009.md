
# Page 9: Little Fox Picking Flowers

## Coloring Page Concept

A cute little fox enjoying a peaceful day in a flower meadow.

The fox should be the main focus and should have a friendly, curious,
and happy expression.

## Main Character

A young, adorable fox sitting among flowers and gently reaching toward
a flower.

The fox should have:

- Large friendly eyes
- Pointed ears
- A small nose
- A gentle smiling mouth
- A fluffy-looking tail with simple outlines
- A cheerful expression

## Adventure Setting

Create a simple meadow scene.

Include:

- Several large flowers
- Simple grass
- A few leaves
- One or two butterflies
- Two fluffy clouds
- A simple distant hill

Keep the background open and uncluttered.

The flowers should be large enough for young children to color easily.

## Composition

Place the fox prominently in the center of the page.

The fox should be sitting and reaching toward one large flower.

Place additional flowers around the fox to create a playful meadow.

Keep the background elements simple and secondary to the fox.

Leave generous open areas for coloring.

## Animal Name

Place the animal's name directly below the fox.

Use:

FOX

The name must be written in large, bold, easy-to-read outlined letters.

The letter interiors must remain white/open so children can color or shade
inside the letters.

Do not use solid black lettering.

The name should be centered beneath the fox and remain comfortably inside
the safe area.

## Illustration Style

- Cute children's coloring-book style
- Black-and-white line art
- Bold, smooth outlines
- Large coloring areas
- Simple shapes
- Friendly facial expression
- Minimal detail
- No gray shading
- No gradients
- White background

## Audience

Children ages 4–8.

## Print Specifications

- Trim size: 8.5 × 11 inches
- Portrait orientation
- No bleed
- Target resolution: 300 DPI
- Target artwork size: 2550 × 3300 pixels
- Keep all important artwork safely inside the safe area

## Restrictions

Do not include:

- Copyrighted characters
- Brand logos
- Trademarked designs
- Watermarks
- Photorealistic elements
- Scary imagery
- Extremely thin lines
- Tiny coloring areas
- Unnecessary text
- Solid black animal-name lettering
