# Page 59: Animal Fun Facts

## Page Purpose
Introduce children to interesting animal facts through a playful, educational coloring activity.

## Main Heading
Display the following words in large, playful, outlined uppercase letters:

AMAZING ANIMAL
FUN FACTS!

## Introduction
Include this short sentence beneath the heading:

Animals are full of surprises!
Discover these amazing animal facts.

Use large, clear lettering suitable for early readers.

## Animal Fact Sections

Arrange five animal fact sections vertically down the page.

Each section should contain:
- One simple animal illustration with bold outlines.
- One short, accurate animal fact.
- Enough white space to keep the layout clear.

### Fact 1: Giraffe

Illustration:
A cute giraffe with a long neck and small spots.

Fact:
A giraffe has a very long neck!

### Fact 2: Penguin

Illustration:
A friendly penguin standing upright.

Fact:
Penguins are birds, but they cannot fly.

### Fact 3: Octopus

Illustration:
A cute octopus with eight clearly visible arms.

Fact:
An octopus has eight arms.

### Fact 4: Elephant

Illustration:
A cheerful elephant with large ears and a trunk.

Fact:
An elephant uses its trunk to pick up food and drink water.

### Fact 5: Butterfly

Illustration:
A butterfly with large, open wings.

Fact:
Butterflies taste with their feet.

## Interactive Activity

At the bottom of the page, include:

WHICH ANIMAL IS YOUR FAVORITE?

Draw a small, empty checkbox beside each answer:

[ ] GIRAFFE

[ ] PENGUIN

[ ] OCTOPUS

[ ] ELEPHANT

[ ] BUTTERFLY

Make the checkboxes large enough for children to mark easily.

## Illustration Rules

- Black-and-white line art only.
- Use bold, clean outlines.
- Leave the animal illustrations uncolored.
- No gray shading, gradients, or colored backgrounds.
- Keep every fact short, accurate, and easy to understand.
- Ensure each illustration clearly matches its fact.
- Avoid overcrowding the page.
- Use readable lettering and generous spacing.
- Do not use complicated scientific vocabulary.

## Print Specifications

- Page size: 8.5 × 11 inches.
- Orientation: Portrait.
- Interior: Black and white.
- Bleed: No bleed.
- Target resolution: 300 DPI.
- Target dimensions: 2550 × 3300 pixels.
- Keep all text and illustrations within safe print margins.
- Leave sufficient white space for coloring and reading.

## Quality Check

Before approving the page, confirm:

- The title is large and easy to read.
- All five animal facts are accurate.
- Every illustration matches the correct animal.
- The octopus has eight arms.
- The checkbox activity is easy to complete.
- No text or illustrations are cut off.
- The page follows the book's 8.5 × 11-inch, no-bleed format.
