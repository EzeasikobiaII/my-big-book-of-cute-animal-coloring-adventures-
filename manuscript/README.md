## Book Concept

A fun and engaging coloring adventure featuring 50 cute animals in simple, imaginative settings.

The book combines animal coloring pages with creative activities designed to encourage children's imagination, creativity, observation, and enjoyment of the animal world.

The illustrations should remain simple enough for children ages 4–8 to color comfortably.

## Illustration Style

Every coloring illustration should use:

- Bold, clean black outlines
- Large coloring areas
- Simple recognizable shapes
- Friendly, child-appropriate expressions
- Clear separation between objects
- Spacious compositions
- Easy-to-color sections
- White background
- No gray
- No gradients
- No shading
- No unnecessary visual clutter

---

## Animal Coloring Page Requirements

Each animal coloring page should:

- Feature one primary animal
- Use a cute and friendly presentation
- Keep the animal clearly recognizable
- Include a simple imaginative environment
- Use large open coloring areas
- Avoid tiny or overly complicated details
- Place the animal name directly below the animal
- Use large bold outlined letters for the animal name
- Keep the inside of the animal-name letters white/open for coloring
- Never use solid-black animal-name lettering
- Keep important content inside the safe area

---

## Activity Page Requirements

Activity pages should be:

- Simple enough for children ages 4–8
- Spacious and easy to understand
- Black-and-white
- Printable
- Designed for coloring, drawing, matching, tracing, or creative participation
- Free from unnecessary clutter
- Consistent with the overall animal adventure theme

## Safe Area

Keep important artwork, text, animal names, borders, and decorative elements comfortably inside the trim edges.

Do not place important content directly against the page edge.

---

## Page Requirements

Each page must be a separate page.

Do not use:

- Two-page spreads
- Contact sheets
- Crop marks
- Trim marks
- Registration marks
- Watermarks
- Placeholder text
- Invisible objects
- Unnecessary metadata

---

## Coloring Artwork

Artwork should be:

- Black line art on a white background
- Bold and clean
- Easy for children ages 4–8 to color
- Simple enough to avoid visual clutter
- Friendly and age appropriate

Avoid:

- Gray shading
- Gradients
- Extremely thin lines
- Tiny coloring areas
- Copyrighted characters
- Brand logos
- Trademarks
- Frightening imagery
- Weapons
- Watermarks

---
