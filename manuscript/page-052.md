# Page 52: Playful Baby Goat on a Sunny Farm

## Animal

Goat

## Scene Description

Create a cute, playful baby goat standing happily in a peaceful farm setting.

The goat should have:
- A small rounded body
- Four short sturdy legs
- A short tail
- A rounded head
- Two small curved horns
- Two floppy ears
- Large friendly eyes
- A small smiling mouth
- A cheerful, curious expression
- Simple recognizable goat features

Keep the goat clearly recognizable while using simple, rounded shapes suitable for children ages 4–8.

Make the goat the main focus of the page with plenty of large open areas for easy coloring.

## Background Elements

Include:
- A simple wooden fence
- Two or three large grass patches
- A small barn in the distance
- A few rounded hay bales
- One small tree
- Two or three fluffy clouds
- A simple sun
- A few small flowers
- One or two butterflies

Keep the farm scene spacious, cheerful, and uncluttered.

## Page Layout

Place the baby goat prominently near the center of the page.

Arrange the fence, barn, hay bales, tree, flowers, and other decorative elements around the goat without crowding the main animal.

Keep all background objects simple and clearly separated.

Place the animal name directly below the goat.

## Illustration Style

- Black-and-white children's coloring book line art
- Bold, smooth outlines
- Large open coloring areas
- Simple rounded shapes suitable for children ages 4–8
- Large, clearly separated sections
- Clear separation between the goat and background elements
- No gray
- No gradients
- No shading
- White background
- Plenty of open space for coloring
- Friendly and cheerful appearance
- Clean printable line art

## Animal Name

Place the word:

**GOAT**

Directly below the goat.

The animal name must:
- Use large, bold, easy-to-read outlined letters
- Have white/open interiors so children can color or shade inside the letters
- NOT be solid black
- Be centered beneath the animal
- Stay safely inside the page margins

## Audience

Kids ages 4–8.

## Print Specifications

- Trim size: 8.5 × 11 inches
- Orientation: Portrait
- Interior: Black-and-white
- Bleed: None
- Target resolution: 300 DPI
- Target artwork dimensions: 2550 × 3300 pixels
- Keep all important artwork and text inside the safe area

## Content Restrictions

Do not include:
- Copyrighted characters
- Trademarked characters
- Logos
- Brand names
- Watermarks
- Photorealistic imagery
- Scary or frightening imagery
- Weapons
- Thin, fragile linework
- Tiny coloring sections
- Unnecessary text
- Solid black animal-name lettering

## Quality Goal

The finished illustration should look like a professional children's coloring-book page: cute, clean, simple, highly printable, and enjoyable for a 4–8-year-old child to color.

The baby goat should be immediately recognizable, adorable, and full of personality while the simple farm setting keeps the page engaging without becoming crowded.
