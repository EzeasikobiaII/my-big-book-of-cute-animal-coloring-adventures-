# Page 1: Title Page

## Title

MY BIG BOOK OF CUTE ANIMAL COLORING ADVENTURES

## Subtitle

50 Fun, Easy & Adorable Coloring Pages for Kids Ages 4–8

## Layout

Portrait, 8.5 × 11 inches.

Centered title area with large, playful, highly readable typography.

Include a simple black-and-white decorative arrangement of cute animal
faces around the title.

Suggested animals:

- Puppy
- Kitten
- Bunny
- Lion Cub
- Elephant
- Panda

Keep the illustration simple and uncluttered.

## Print Requirements

- Black-and-white
- 300 DPI
- No bleed
- No copyrighted characters
- No brand logos
- No unnecessary decorative clutter
- Text must remain comfortably inside the safe area
