
# Page 8: Baby Bear Exploring the Woods

## Coloring Page Concept

A cute, friendly baby bear exploring a peaceful woodland.

The bear should be the main focus and should look curious, happy, and
adventurous.

## Main Character

A young, adorable baby bear walking through the woods.

The bear should have:

- Large friendly eyes
- Small rounded ears
- A tiny nose
- A gentle smiling mouth
- A cheerful expression
- Large, simple paws

## Adventure Setting

Create a simple woodland scene.

Include:

- Two or three simple trees
- A few large leaves
- Two or three simple mushrooms
- Small patches of grass
- A few simple flowers
- One or two fluffy clouds visible between the trees

Keep the woodland peaceful and uncluttered.

All objects should have large, easy-to-color sections.

## Composition

Place the baby bear prominently in the center of the page.

The bear should be walking forward as though exploring the woods.

Place the trees toward the sides of the composition so they frame the bear
without overwhelming it.

Keep generous open spaces throughout the illustration.

## Animal Name

Place the animal's name directly below the bear.

Use:

BEAR

The name must be written in large, bold, easy-to-read outlined letters.

The letter interiors must remain white/open so children can color or shade
inside the letters.

Do not use solid black lettering.

The name should be centered beneath the bear and remain comfortably inside
the safe area.

## Illustration Style

- Cute children's coloring-book style
- Black-and-white line art
- Bold, smooth outlines
- Large coloring areas
- Simple shapes
- Friendly facial expression
- Minimal detail
- No gray shading
- No gradients
- White background

## Audience

Children ages 4–8.

## Print Specifications

- Trim size: 8.5 × 11 inches
- Portrait orientation
- No bleed
- Target resolution: 300 DPI
- Target artwork size: 2550 × 3300 pixels
- Keep all important artwork safely inside the safe area

## Restrictions

Do not include:

- Copyrighted characters
- Brand logos
- Trademarked designs
- Watermarks
- Photorealistic elements
- Scary imagery
- Extremely thin lines
- Tiny coloring areas
- Unnecessary text
- Solid black animal-name lettering
