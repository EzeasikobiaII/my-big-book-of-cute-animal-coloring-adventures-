# Page 44: Busy Beaver by the River

## Animal
Beaver

## Scene Description
Create a cute, friendly beaver sitting happily beside a peaceful forest river.

The beaver should have:
- A rounded body
- Large friendly eyes
- Small rounded ears
- A broad, rounded nose
- Two prominent front teeth
- Small front paws
- Short sturdy legs
- A large flat paddle-shaped tail
- A cheerful, friendly expression

Keep the beaver clearly recognizable while using simple shapes suitable for children ages 4–8.

## Background Elements
Include:
- A calm river with gentle curved water lines
- A few smooth river stones
- Several simple trees
- A small beaver dam made from logs and branches
- Large leaves and bushes
- A few simple water plants
- Two or three butterflies
- A small wooden branch floating near the riverbank

Keep the riverside scene spacious and uncluttered.

## Illustration Style
- Black-and-white children's coloring book line art
- Bold, smooth outlines
- Large open coloring areas
- Simple shapes suitable for children ages 4–8
- Large, clearly separated sections
- No gray
- No gradients
- No shading
- White background
- Clear separation between objects
- Plenty of open space for coloring

## Animal Name

Place the word:

**BEAVER**

Directly below the beaver.

The animal name must:
- Use large, bold, easy-to-read outlined letters
- Have white/open interiors so children can color or shade inside the letters
- NOT be solid black
- Be centered beneath the animal
- Stay safely inside the page margins

## Audience
Kids ages 4–8.

## Print Specifications
- Trim size: 8.5 × 11 inches
- Orientation: Portrait
- Interior: Black-and-white
- Bleed: None
- Target resolution: 300 DPI
- Target artwork dimensions: 2550 × 3300 pixels
- Keep all important artwork and text inside the safe area

## Content Restrictions
Do not include:
- Copyrighted characters
- Trademarked characters
- Logos
- Brand names
- Watermarks
- Photorealistic imagery
- Scary or frightening imagery
- Weapons
- Thin, fragile linework
- Tiny coloring sections
- Unnecessary text
- Solid black animal-name lettering

## Quality Goal
The finished illustration should look like a professional children's coloring-book page: cute, clean, simple, highly printable, and enjoyable for a 4–8-year-old child to color.
