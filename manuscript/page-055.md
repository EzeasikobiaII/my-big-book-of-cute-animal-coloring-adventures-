# Page 55: My Favorite Animal

## Activity

Create a fun, spacious activity page where children can draw and color their favorite animal from the book.

The page should encourage creativity, self-expression, and early writing skills. Children should be able to complete the activity whether they prefer drawing, writing, or both.

## Main Heading

Include the heading:

MY FAVORITE ANIMAL

Place the heading near the top of the page using large, bold, easy-to-read outlined letters.

The letter interiors must remain white and open so children can color them.

Do not use solid-black lettering.

## Drawing Area

Below the heading, create a large blank drawing area surrounded by a simple decorative border.

The drawing area should:
- Occupy a generous portion of the page.
- Have a clear, easy-to-follow border.
- Provide enough room for a child to draw a complete animal.
- Remain mostly empty in the center.
- Have rounded corners or other simple, child-friendly design elements.

Include a few small decorative elements around the border, such as:
- Stars
- Hearts
- Small leaves
- Tiny flowers
- Simple clouds
- Small paw prints

Keep the decorations near the edges so they do not interfere with the drawing area.

Do not fill the center with decorative objects.

## Favorite Animal Name

Below the drawing area, include the heading:

MY FAVORITE ANIMAL IS:

Place a long, clear writing line underneath the heading.

The line should provide enough space for a child to write the name of their favorite animal.

Keep the text large, readable, and comfortably spaced.

## Why I Love My Animal

Below the animal-name section, include the heading:

I LOVE IT BECAUSE:

Provide two or three wide, evenly spaced writing lines.

The lines should:
- Be widely spaced for young children's handwriting.
- Be long enough for short answers.
- Remain clearly visible in black-and-white printing.

Leave enough open space for children who prefer to draw their answer instead of writing.

Do not crowd this section or make the writing lines too small.

## Page Layout

Arrange the page in this order:

1. Large outlined heading at the top.
2. Large central drawing area with a simple decorative border.
3. Favorite-animal heading and one long writing line.
4. Why-I-love-it heading and two or three wide writing lines.

Maintain a balanced layout with generous spacing between sections.

All text, borders, and decorations must remain within the safe print margins.

## Illustration Style

- Black-and-white children's activity-book line art
- Bold, smooth outlines
- Large open areas
- Simple decorative shapes
- Clear separation between decorative elements
- White background
- No gray shading
- No gradients
- No shadows
- No unnecessary tiny details
- Plenty of writing and drawing space
- Easy for children ages 4–8 to use

## Audience

Children ages 4–8.

The activity should be accessible to early learners who may not yet be able to write complete sentences.

Children should be able to participate by drawing, coloring, writing a few words, or combining these activities.

## Print Specifications

- Trim size: 8.5 × 11 inches
- Orientation: Portrait
- Interior: Black-and-white
- Bleed: No bleed
- Target resolution: 300 DPI
- Target artwork dimensions: 2550 × 3300 pixels
- Keep all important artwork and text inside the safe area
- Use clear, printable lines
- Maintain generous margins
- Ensure all writing lines remain visible in the final printed page

## Content Restrictions

Do not include:
- Copyrighted characters
- Trademarked characters
- Logos
- Brand names
- Watermarks
- Photorealistic imagery
- Scary or frightening imagery
- Weapons
- Tiny writing areas
- Crowded layouts
- Excessive decorative elements
- Solid-black decorative lettering
- Gray shading
- Gradients
- Unnecessary instructional text
