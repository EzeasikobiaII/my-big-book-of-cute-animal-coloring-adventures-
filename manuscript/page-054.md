# Page 54: Gentle Elephant by the Watering Hole

## Animal

Elephant

## Scene Description

Create a cute, friendly elephant standing peacefully beside a small watering hole.

The elephant should have:
- A large rounded body
- Four sturdy legs
- A long curved trunk
- Two large fan-shaped ears
- Small friendly eyes
- Two simple tusks
- A short tail with a small tuft
- A cheerful, gentle expression

Keep the elephant clearly recognizable while using simple, rounded shapes suitable for children ages 4–8.

Make the elephant the main focus of the page with large open areas that are easy for children to color.

## Background Elements

Include:
- A small peaceful watering hole
- Two or three large smooth rocks
- A few simple grass patches
- One small acacia tree
- Several large leaves
- Two or three fluffy clouds
- A simple sun
- A few small birds in the distance

Keep the scene spacious, peaceful, and uncluttered.

## Page Layout

Place the elephant prominently near the center of the page.

Position the watering hole slightly beside the elephant so both remain clearly visible.

Arrange the tree, rocks, grass, leaves, clouds, and birds around the main animal without crowding it.

Keep all objects large enough to provide comfortable coloring areas.

Place the animal name directly below the elephant.

## Illustration Style

- Black-and-white children's coloring book line art
- Bold, smooth outlines
- Large open coloring areas
- Simple rounded shapes suitable for children ages 4–8
- Large, clearly separated sections
- Simple recognizable elephant features
- No gray
- No gradients
- No shading
- White background
- Clear separation between objects
- Plenty of open space for coloring
- Friendly and cheerful appearance
- Clean printable line art

## Animal Name

Place the word:

**ELEPHANT**

Directly below the elephant.

The animal name must:
- Use large, bold, easy-to-read outlined letters
- Have white/open interiors so children can color or shade inside the letters
- NOT be solid black
- Be centered beneath the animal
- Stay safely inside the page margins

## Audience

Kids ages 4–8.

## Print Specifications

- Trim size: 8.5 × 11 inches
- Orientation: Portrait
- Interior: Black-and-white
- Bleed: None
- Target resolution: 300 DPI
- Target artwork dimensions: 2550 × 3300 pixels
- Keep all important artwork and text inside the safe area

## Content Restrictions

Do not include:
- Copyrighted characters
- Trademarked characters
- Logos
- Brand names
- Watermarks
- Photorealistic imagery
- Scary or frightening imagery
- Weapons
- Thin, fragile linework
- Tiny coloring sections
- Unnecessary text
- Solid black animal-name lettering

## Quality Goal

The finished illustration should look like a professional children's coloring-book page: cute, clean, simple, highly printable, and enjoyable for a 4–8-year-old child to color.

The elephant should be immediately recognizable, gentle, and inviting, while the watering-hole setting adds variety to the animal adventure and provides several large, easy-to-color background elements.
