# Page 53: Happy Seal by the Seaside

## Animal

Seal

## Scene Description

Create a cute, friendly seal sitting happily beside a peaceful seaside shore.

The seal should have:
- A rounded, smooth body
- A rounded head
- Large friendly eyes
- Small rounded ears
- A short, simple snout
- Two small front flippers
- Two simple rear flippers
- A small smiling mouth
- A cheerful, playful expression

Keep the seal clearly recognizable while using simple, rounded shapes suitable for children ages 4–8.

Make the seal the main focus of the page with large open areas that are easy for children to color.

## Background Elements

Include:
- A simple sandy shoreline
- Two or three large smooth seashells
- A few rounded rocks
- Simple gentle ocean waves
- One small beach umbrella
- Two or three fluffy clouds
- A simple sun
- A few small flying birds in the distance
- A small starfish near the shoreline

Keep the seaside scene spacious, peaceful, and uncluttered.

## Page Layout

Place the seal prominently near the center of the page.

Arrange the seashells, rocks, waves, umbrella, clouds, and other decorative elements around the seal without crowding the main animal.

Keep the ocean waves simple and clearly separated.

Place the animal name directly below the seal.

## Illustration Style

- Black-and-white children's coloring book line art
- Bold, smooth outlines
- Large open coloring areas
- Simple rounded shapes suitable for children ages 4–8
- Large, clearly separated sections
- Clear separation between the seal and background elements
- No gray
- No gradients
- No shading
- White background
- Plenty of open space for coloring
- Friendly and cheerful appearance
- Clean printable line art

## Animal Name

Place the word:

**SEAL**

Directly below the seal.

The animal name must:
- Use large, bold, easy-to-read outlined letters
- Have white/open interiors so children can color or shade inside the letters
- NOT be solid black
- Be centered beneath the animal
- Stay safely inside the page margins

## Audience

Kids ages 4–8.

## Print Specifications

- Trim size: 8.5 × 11 inches
- Orientation: Portrait
- Interior: Black-and-white
- Bleed: None
- Target resolution: 300 DPI
- Target artwork dimensions: 2550 × 3300 pixels
- Keep all important artwork and text inside the safe area

## Content Restrictions

Do not include:
- Copyrighted characters
- Trademarked characters
- Logos
- Brand names
- Watermarks
- Photorealistic imagery
- Scary or frightening imagery
- Weapons
- Thin, fragile linework
- Tiny coloring sections
- Unnecessary text
- Solid black animal-name lettering

## Quality Goal

The finished illustration should look like a professional children's coloring-book page: cute, clean, simple, highly printable, and enjoyable for a 4–8-year-old child to color.

The seal should be immediately recognizable, adorable, and playful, while the simple seaside setting adds an inviting new environment to the animal adventure.
