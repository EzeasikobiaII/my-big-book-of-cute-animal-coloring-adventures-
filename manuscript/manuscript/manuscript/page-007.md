# Page 7: Bunny Having a Picnic

## Coloring Page Concept

A cute, happy bunny enjoying a peaceful picnic outdoors.

The bunny should be the main focus of the illustration and should have a
friendly, cheerful expression.

## Main Character

A young, adorable bunny sitting beside a picnic basket.

The bunny should have:

- Large friendly eyes
- Long floppy ears
- A small nose
- A gentle smiling mouth
- A cheerful expression
- Simple paws and feet

## Adventure Setting

Create a simple outdoor picnic scene.

Include:

- A picnic basket
- A small picnic blanket
- Two or three pieces of simple fruit
- A few large flowers
- Simple grass
- One or two fluffy clouds
- A small butterfly

Keep the background simple and uncluttered.

All objects should have large, easy-to-color sections.

## Composition

Place the bunny prominently near the center of the page.

The picnic basket should be positioned beside the bunny.

The picnic blanket should provide a simple base for the scene.

Place the flowers, clouds, and butterfly around the main scene without
crowding the page.

Leave generous open areas for coloring.

## Animal Name

Place the animal's name directly below the bunny.

Use:

BUNNY

The name must be written in large, bold, easy-to-read outlined letters.

The letter interiors must remain white/open so children can color or shade
inside the letters.

Do not use solid black lettering.

The name should be centered beneath the bunny and remain comfortably inside
the safe area.

## Illustration Style

- Cute children's coloring-book style
- Black-and-white line art
- Bold, smooth outlines
- Large coloring areas
- Simple shapes
- Friendly facial expression
- Minimal detail
- No gray shading
- No gradients
- White background

## Audience

Children ages 4–8.

## Print Specifications

- Trim size: 8.5 × 11 inches
- Portrait orientation
- No bleed
- Target resolution: 300 DPI
- Target artwork size: 2550 × 3300 pixels
- Keep all important artwork safely inside the safe area

## Restrictions

Do not include:

- Copyrighted characters
- Brand logos
- Trademarked designs
- Watermarks
- Photorealistic elements
- Scary imagery
- Extremely thin lines
- Tiny coloring areas
- Unnecessary text
- Solid black animal-name lettering
