# Page 6: Cute Kitten Playing With Yarn

## Coloring Page Concept

A cute, playful kitten having fun with a large ball of yarn.

The kitten should be the main focus and should look friendly, curious,
and happy.

## Main Character

A young, adorable kitten sitting and playing with yarn.

The kitten should have:

- Large friendly eyes
- Small triangular ears
- A tiny smiling mouth
- A cheerful expression
- Simple whiskers
- A simple collar without any logo or brand

## Adventure Setting

Create a simple cozy indoor setting around the kitten.

Include:

- One large ball of yarn
- A few simple curved yarn strands
- A small cushion
- A simple floor area
- One or two small decorative shapes

Keep the setting uncluttered.

The yarn should have large, simple sections that are easy for children to
color.

## Composition

Place the kitten prominently in the center of the page.

The large ball of yarn should be beside the kitten.

Keep the kitten's body large enough to provide generous coloring areas.

Avoid filling the page with excessive background details.

## Animal Name

Place the animal's name directly below the kitten.

Use:

KITTEN

The name must be written in large, bold, easy-to-read outlined letters.

The letter interiors must remain white/open so children can color or shade
inside the letters.

Do not use solid black lettering.

The name should be centered beneath the kitten and remain comfortably inside
the safe area.

## Illustration Style

- Cute children's coloring-book style
- Black-and-white line art
- Bold, smooth outlines
- Large coloring areas
- Simple shapes
- Friendly facial expression
- Minimal detail
- No gray shading
- No gradients
- White background

## Audience

Children ages 4–8.

## Print Specifications

- Trim size: 8.5 × 11 inches
- Portrait orientation
- No bleed
- Target resolution: 300 DPI
- Target artwork size: 2550 × 3300 pixels
- Keep all important artwork safely inside the safe area

## Restrictions

Do not include:

- Copyrighted characters
- Brand logos
- Trademarked designs
- Watermarks
- Photorealistic elements
- Scary imagery
- Extremely thin lines
- Tiny coloring areas
- Unnecessary text
- Solid black animal-name lettering
