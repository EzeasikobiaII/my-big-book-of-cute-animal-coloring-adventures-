# Page 4: Welcome to the Animal Adventure!

## Main Heading

LET'S GO ON AN ANIMAL ADVENTURE!

## Page Text

Grab your crayons, pencils, or markers and get ready to explore!

Meet playful puppies, curious jungle animals, cuddly forest friends, and
amazing creatures from the ocean.

There are no wrong colors here. Use your imagination and make every animal
your own!

## Decorative Illustration

Create a simple black-and-white arrangement of friendly animal faces.

Suggested animals:

- Puppy
- Kitten
- Lion Cub
- Elephant
- Panda
- Dolphin

The animals should have happy, friendly expressions.

## Print Requirements

- 8.5 × 11 inches
- Portrait orientation
- No bleed
- Black-and-white
- 300 DPI final artwork
- Bold, clean outlines
- Large open areas
- Easy to read for children ages 4–8
- No copyrighted characters
- No logos or trademarks
- Keep all text and artwork inside the safe area
