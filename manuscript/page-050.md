# Page 50: Curious Fennec Fox in the Desert

## Animal
Fennec Fox

## Scene Description
Create a cute, friendly fennec fox standing on a gentle sand dune in a peaceful desert landscape.

This page must look clearly different from the regular fox on page 9. The fennec fox must have exceptionally large ears, a compact body, and a desert setting. Do not reuse the flower meadow, flower-picking pose, or composition from page 9.

## Main Character
The fennec fox should have:
- Very large, distinctive ears that are its most recognizable feature
- A small, rounded body
- Large, friendly eyes
- A short, pointed muzzle
- A tiny, rounded nose
- Four small legs
- A fluffy tail
- A cheerful, curious expression

Keep the animal recognizable and easy for children ages 4–8 to color. Avoid fine fur textures and tiny details.

## Background Elements
Include:
- Two or three broad, rounded desert dunes
- One or two simple cacti
- A few desert plants
- Two or three smooth desert rocks
- A few large, scattered pebbles
- A simple palm tree in the distance
- One or two fluffy clouds

Keep the desert scene spacious and uncluttered. Do not use a flower meadow or dense flower background.

## Composition
Place the fennec fox prominently in the center, standing on a gentle sand dune.

Make its oversized ears clearly visible and separate from the background. Keep the animal large enough for comfortable coloring. Leave generous open areas throughout the page.

## Animal Name

Place the words:

FENNEC FOX

Directly below the animal.

Use large, bold, easy-to-read outlined letters with white interiors so children can color inside them. Do not use solid-black lettering. Center the name and keep it safely inside the page margins.

## Illustration Style
- Black-and-white children's coloring-book line art
- Bold, smooth outlines
- Large open coloring areas
- Simple shapes suitable for children ages 4–8
- Clearly separated objects
- No gray shading
- No gradients
- No shadows
- White background
- Plenty of open space for coloring

## Audience
Children ages 4–8.

## Print Specifications
- Trim size: 8.5 × 11 inches
- Orientation: Portrait
- Interior: Black-and-white
- Bleed: None
- Target resolution: 300 DPI
- Target artwork dimensions: 2550 × 3300 pixels
- Keep all important artwork and text inside the safe area

## Content Restrictions
Do not include copyrighted characters, logos, trademarks, watermarks, photorealistic imagery, frightening imagery, weapons, fragile linework, tiny coloring sections, unnecessary text, or solid-black animal-name lettering.

## Quality Goal
The finished illustration should be a clean, friendly, printable coloring page featuring a distinctive desert animal, oversized ears, and large, easy-to-color shapes.
