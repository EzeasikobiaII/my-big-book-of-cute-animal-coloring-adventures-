# Page 57: My Animal Adventure Certificate

## Activity
Create a fun and celebratory certificate page for children who have completed their animal coloring adventure.

## Page Layout
Include:

**MY ANIMAL ADVENTURE CERTIFICATE**

Place the heading near the top of the page using large, bold, outlined letters with white/open interiors so children can color the letters.

Below the heading, include a short message:

**This certificate belongs to:**

Follow this with a long blank line where the child can write their name.

Then include:

**For completing the Animal Coloring Adventure!**

Add a large decorative certificate frame around the main content.

Inside the frame, include simple animal-themed decorations such as:
- Paw prints
- Stars
- Hearts
- Leaves
- Small animal footprints
- Simple flowers

Near the bottom, include:

**Date:** ______________________

**Signature:** ______________________

## Decorative Illustration
Include a few small, generic cute animal faces around the certificate border.

The animals should be original and simple, with no recognizable copyrighted characters.

Keep the center of the certificate clean and easy to read.

## Illustration Style
- Black-and-white children's activity-book line art
- Bold, smooth outlines
- Large open coloring areas
- Simple decorative shapes
- No gray
- No gradients
- No shading
- White background
- Clear separation between decorative elements
- Plenty of open space for coloring
- Easy for children ages 4–8 to use

## Audience
Kids ages 4–8.

## Print Specifications
- Trim size: 8.5 × 11 inches
- Orientation: Portrait
- Interior: Black-and-white
- Bleed: None
- Target resolution: 300 DPI
- Target artwork dimensions: 2550 × 3300 pixels
- Keep all important artwork and text inside the safe area

## Content Restrictions
Do not include:
- Copyrighted characters
- Trademarked characters
- Logos
- Brand names
- Watermarks
- Photorealistic imagery
- Scary or frightening imagery
- Weapons
- Tiny writing areas
- Crowded layouts
- Excessive decorative elements
- Solid black lettering

## Quality Goal
The finished page should feel like a special reward for completing the coloring adventure, while giving the child a certificate they can proudly color, personalize, and keep.
