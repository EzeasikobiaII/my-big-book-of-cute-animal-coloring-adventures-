# Page 57: My Animal Adventure Certificate

## Page Purpose
A cheerful completion certificate celebrating the child's animal coloring adventure.

## Main Heading
Display the following words in large, playful, outlined uppercase letters:

MY ANIMAL ADVENTURE
CERTIFICATE

## Certificate Layout

Create a beautiful, child-friendly certificate with a large decorative rectangular border.

Use simple black outlines only. Keep the layout spacious, balanced, and easy to read.

### Main Certificate Text

Include the following text in a clear, friendly font:

THIS CERTIFICATE IS AWARDED TO:

Provide one long, blank line where the child can write their name.

### Achievement Message

Display the following message:

FOR COMPLETING
MY BIG BOOK OF CUTE ANIMAL
COLORING ADVENTURES!

### Date Section

Include:

DATE: __________________________

### Decorative Elements

Add small, simple outline illustrations around the certificate border, such as:

- A smiling puppy
- A cute bunny
- A little bird
- A star
- A heart
- Small paw prints

Keep all decorations near the edges so the certificate text remains easy to read.

The animals should be simple, cute, and suitable for children ages 4–8.

## Illustration Rules

- Black-and-white line art only.
- Use bold, clean outlines.
- Leave all illustrations uncolored so children can color them.
- Do not use gray shading, gradients, or solid black backgrounds.
- Do not overcrowd the page.
- Keep the recipient's name and date lines completely blank.
- Ensure every word is correctly spelled and readable.
- Do not include a real signature, real person's name, or pre-filled date.

## Print Specifications

- Page size: 8.5 × 11 inches.
- Orientation: Portrait.
- Interior: Black and white.
- Bleed: No bleed.
- Target resolution: 300 DPI.
- Target dimensions: 2550 × 3300 pixels.
- Keep all text, borders, and illustrations within safe print margins.
- Leave sufficient white space for writing and coloring.

## Quality Check

Before approving the page, confirm:

- The certificate heading is large and readable.
- The child's name line is long enough for handwriting.
- The date line is clearly visible.
- The decorative illustrations do not interfere with the text.
- The page contains no unwanted shading or color.
- The design is appropriate for children ages 4–8.
- The page follows the book's 8.5 × 11-inch, no-bleed format.
