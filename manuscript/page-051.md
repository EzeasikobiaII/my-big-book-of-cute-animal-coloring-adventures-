# Page 51: Cheerful Zebra on the Savannah

## Animal

Zebra

## Scene Description

Create a cute, friendly zebra standing happily in an open savannah landscape.

The zebra should have:
- A rounded body
- Four sturdy legs
- A short tail with a simple tuft
- A rounded head
- Large friendly eyes
- Upright ears
- A short flowing mane
- A cheerful, gentle expression
- Clearly recognizable zebra stripes

Keep the zebra clearly recognizable while using simple, rounded shapes suitable for children ages 4–8.

Make the zebra the main focus of the page with large open areas that are easy for children to color.

## Background Elements

Include:
- Two or three simple savannah grass patches
- One small acacia tree
- A few large smooth rocks
- Two or three simple clouds
- A small sun
- A few distant grass tufts
- One or two simple butterflies

Keep the savannah scene spacious, peaceful, and uncluttered.

## Page Layout

Place the zebra prominently near the center of the page.

Arrange the acacia tree, rocks, grass, clouds, and other decorative elements around the zebra without crowding the main animal.

Keep the zebra's stripes bold, smooth, and clearly separated so children can easily color between them.

Place the animal name directly below the zebra.

## Illustration Style

- Black-and-white children's coloring book line art
- Bold, smooth outlines
- Large open coloring areas
- Simple shapes suitable for children ages 4–8
- Large, clearly separated sections
- Bold, easy-to-color zebra stripes
- No gray
- No gradients
- No shading
- White background
- Clear separation between objects
- Plenty of open space for coloring
- Friendly and cheerful appearance
- Clean printable line art

## Animal Name

Place the word:

**ZEBRA**

Directly below the zebra.

The animal name must:
- Use large, bold, easy-to-read outlined letters
- Have white/open interiors so children can color or shade inside the letters
- NOT be solid black
- Be centered beneath the animal
- Stay safely inside the page margins

## Audience

Kids ages 4–8.

## Print Specifications

- Trim size: 8.5 × 11 inches
- Orientation: Portrait
- Interior: Black-and-white
- Bleed: None
- Target resolution: 300 DPI
- Target artwork dimensions: 2550 × 3300 pixels
- Keep all important artwork and text inside the safe area

## Content Restrictions

Do not include:
- Copyrighted characters
- Trademarked characters
- Logos
- Brand names
- Watermarks
- Photorealistic imagery
- Scary or frightening imagery
- Weapons
- Thin, fragile linework
- Tiny coloring sections
- Unnecessary text
- Solid black animal-name lettering

## Quality Goal

The finished illustration should look like a professional children's coloring-book page: cute, clean, simple, highly printable, and enjoyable for a 4–8-year-old child to color.

The zebra should be immediately recognizable, with bold, spacious stripes and a cheerful savannah setting that fits naturally into the animal adventure theme.
