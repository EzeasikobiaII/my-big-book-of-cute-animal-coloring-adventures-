# Page 51: My Favorite Animal

## Activity
Create a fun, spacious activity page where children can draw and color their favorite animal from the book.

## Page Layout
Include:

**MY FAVORITE ANIMAL**

Place the heading near the top of the page using large, bold, outlined letters with white/open interiors so children can color the letters.

Below the heading, include a large blank drawing area with a simple decorative border.

Inside the drawing area, include a few small, simple decorative elements such as:
- Stars
- Hearts
- Small leaves
- Tiny flowers
- Simple clouds

Keep the center of the drawing area mostly empty so children have plenty of room to draw.

Near the bottom of the page, include:

**My favorite animal is:**

Follow this with a long blank line where the child can write the animal's name.

Also include:

**I love it because:**

Follow this with two or three wide writing lines for the child to write or draw their answer.

## Illustration Style
- Black-and-white children's activity-book line art
- Bold, smooth outlines
- Large open areas
- Simple decorative shapes
- No gray
- No gradients
- No shading
- White background
- Plenty of writing and drawing space
- Easy for children ages 4–8 to use

## Audience
Kids ages 4–8.

## Print Specifications
- Trim size: 8.5 × 11 inches
- Orientation: Portrait
- Interior: Black-and-white
- Bleed: None
- Target resolution: 300 DPI
- Target artwork dimensions: 2550 × 3300 pixels
- Keep all important artwork and text inside the safe area

## Content Restrictions
Do not include:
- Copyrighted characters
- Trademarked characters
- Logos
- Brand names
- Watermarks
- Photorealistic imagery
- Scary or frightening imagery
- Weapons
- Tiny writing areas
- Crowded layouts
- Excessive decorative elements
- Solid black decorative lettering

## Quality Goal
The finished page should feel like a fun creative break after the animal coloring pages, giving children plenty of space to draw, write, and express their favorite animal.
